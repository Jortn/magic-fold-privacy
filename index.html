This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Magic Fold mobile game.

The developer does not directly collect or maintain a database of players' personal information through the game. However, third-party services integrated into the game may collect and process information as described below.

1. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

Magic Fold uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Depending on the app configuration, your device, and your location, Google AdMob may collect or process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID where available;
  • information about app use and interactions with advertisements;
  • device, operating system, and diagnostic information; and
  • information used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation.

The developer does not independently operate these advertising data systems. Google's processing is subject to Google's policies and the settings and consent choices applicable to the app.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and advertising policies.

2. How Information May Be Used

Information processed by Google AdMob may be used to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and maintain or improve advertising services. Which purposes apply can depend on your location, consent choices, and the app's configuration.

3. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell players' personal information. Google and its advertising partners may process or share information as described in Google's policies, including to provide advertising, measure performance, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements.

4. Data Retention and Security

The developer does not maintain a separate database of personal information collected through Magic Fold. Information processed by Google AdMob is handled according to Google's retention and security practices. For details, please consult Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

Magic Fold is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. The app's target audience, age rating, and advertising settings should be configured appropriately. If the app is directed to children or is made available to them, additional requirements for consent, data protection, and advertising may apply.

6. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your device, location, and applicable law, you may be able to manage advertising preferences in your Android settings or through Google's advertising controls. You may also be shown consent choices in the app where required and configured.

Visit Google My Ad Center for information about Google's advertising controls.

7. Third-Party Services

Magic Fold uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. The developer does not control all data processing performed by Google or its advertising partners. Their services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.
